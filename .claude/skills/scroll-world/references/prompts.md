# Prompt Templates & Intake

## Core Process

The workflow follows seven steps, beginning with an **intake checklist** that collects: business description, brand name, color palette, tone, art style, ordered sections, camera approach, mobile requirements, video tier, and image source.

## Style Foundation

All scenes use an identical **style preamble** as the cohesion anchor. The default is: "Isometric low-poly 3D diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain solid [BG_HEX] background with a soft contact shadow beneath it."

Five alternate aesthetic directions:
- Flat papercraft
- Glossy toy
- Claymation
- Neon night
- Photoreal architectural

Each swaps the opening sentences while preserving the palette and no-text requirements.

## Scene Architecture

Two main filming approaches:

- **Architecture A (walkthrough)**: One continuous forward camera flight across connected scenes
- **Locked-iso option**: Fixed isometric angle throughout, eliminating rotation

Three clip types build the final sequence: dive-in (entering each diorama), legs (forward movement between sections), and connectors (aerial transitions between scenes).

## Technical Specifications

Scene stills render at `3:2` aspect ratio, `2k` resolution, `high` quality. Video clips specify either Seedance (1080p, 8–10 second duration) or Kling3_0 parameters. Mobile delivery doubles the credit cost but generates native 9:16 portrait renders for each dive and connector.

## Copy Structure

Each section requires an eyebrow label, 3–6 word title, one-sentence body text, and 0–3 proof tags—with the final section serving as the hero product pitch and call-to-action.
